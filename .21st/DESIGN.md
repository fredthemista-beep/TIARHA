# TIARHA design context

## Product

- Bureau RH territorial SaaS
- Next.js 14, React 18, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/Base UI and Lucide icons
- Light workspace with dark institutional navigation
- Comfortable operational density

## Visual direction

- Deep navy navigation: `#0A172C`
- Primary action blue: `#3974D8`
- Restrained attention coral: `#E66C5C`
- Compliance and positive mint: `#57A88A`
- Page background: `#F1F5F7`
- White card surfaces with subtle navy-tinted shadows
- Geist for UI, Geist Mono for identifiers and numeric detail
- Controls use 8–12 px radii; cards use 14–20 px radii

## Product rules

- Keep French public-sector HR terminology and legal trust signals.
- Make daily decisions and deadlines visible before detailed tables.
- Remain usable from 360 px mobile to large desktop.
- Preserve keyboard focus, semantic headings and reduced-motion support.
- Use coral only for alerts and strong emphasis.
- Avoid emoji navigation, decorative gradients and unnecessary card grids.

## Durable decision

On 2026-09-27, the product adopted a dark institutional navigation rail, a light workspace, rounded operational cards, a restrained coral accent and a priority rail. The direction is grounded in the four SaaS dashboard references supplied by the user and in TIARHA's existing legal and HR vocabulary.
